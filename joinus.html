Why a challenge?

The challenge cuts through noise and shows who can apply knowledge under pressure. It also reflects our values: creativity, integrity, and operational rigor.

High-level overview

  1. Apply: Submit your CV and details to receive access to the challenge link.
  2. Sandboxed challenge: Complete the task inside a secure, isolated environment or provided puzzle files. No live systems are targeted.
  3. Prove your process: Provide a clear write-up (and where relevant, reproducible scripts or a short walk-through video).
  4. Verification: Our team reviews submissions. Accepted candidates receive next steps and our HR contact via direct email.

FAQ

Can I attempt to break other Renngang systems?
No. Only interact with the provided sandbox. Unauthorized activity will lead to disqualification and possible legal action.
What if I find a vulnerability in the sandbox?
Report immediately through the responsible disclosure channel. Don’t exploit it.
How long is the challenge valid?
Each challenge window is time-boxed and will be stated up-front on the application page.
