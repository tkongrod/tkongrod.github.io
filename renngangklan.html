About Renngang

The Renngang klan

Renngang is the place where Classic Meets the Future

Born in the Norwegian Mountains High in the norske fjell, where precision meets passion and innovation races with nature, Renngang is rewriting the story of the Classic Mini. From the heart of Norway’s emerging tech scene, we fuse elektrifisering, cutting-edge engineering, and racing DNA to take the Mini to entirely new heights. Grounded in the strength of norsk industri and driven by a vision of sustainable performance, we blend heritage mechanics with the most advanced tools, materials, and technologies the future has to offer. This isn’t just restoration, it’s transformation.
