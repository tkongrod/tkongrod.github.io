About Renngang

The Renngang team members

Renngang is the place where Engineers meet for a refuel.

At Renngang Engineering, we are a team of elite professionals with backgrounds spanning special forces engineering, mechanical and software engineering, and advanced robotics. What sets us apart is not only our diverse technical expertise, but the mindset we bring. We are mission driven, precision-focused, and committed to excellence.

Our team includes former special operations engineers who’ve applied engineering principles in the most demanding conditions on earth. We've combined that hands on experience with deep expertise in mechanical systems, software development, and robotics innovation. We build everything from battlefield ready systems to high performance manufacturing platforms.

Today, we’ve brought that experience together to build something bigger. We are a next generation education company that delivers world class engineering courses to people who demand more. Our programs are designed to meet or exceed the standards of the best technical universities but without the gatekeeping, bureaucracy, or crushing tuition costs. Whether you're pivoting into engineering, leveling up your skills, or pursuing the knowledge that traditional education never made accessible, we’re here to provide a clear, practical, and high-level path forward. Renngang Engineering is built by operators, designed for engineers and most important Open to Anyone.
