The Renngang Team

Renngang is an engineering education initiative based in Norway. We create clear, exam focused courses for engineering students who want to truly understand the fundamentals, not just memorize formulas.

Renngang Engineering is forged by professionals from mechanical and software systems, autonomous robotics, and special aviation forces engineering.

Our instructors have backgrounds in mechanical and robotics engineering, with real world industry experience in energy, robotics, and advanced systems.
